First release on Google Play.
• Stream your Windows, macOS or Linux computer to your phone with low latency, sound included
• Virtual trackpad, full keyboard, a remote shell and file transfer both ways
• Connect in seconds: scan the QR code the computer shows, or type its address and have its owner press Approve
• Encrypted end to end between your own devices — no accounts, no servers
• The computer needs Deskhub 9 or newer, free from the project page

Deskhub streams the screen of your Windows, macOS or Linux computer to your iPhone or iPad and lets you control it with ultra-low latency — built for gaming, not just remote work.

FEATURES
• Live H.264 video stream with hardware decoding
• Hear what the remote computer is playing, in step with the picture
• Virtual trackpad: drag anywhere to move the mouse, tap to click, double-tap to right-click, hold and drag to select
• Pinch to zoom into the remote screen; a two-finger drag scrolls, or pans once you are zoomed in
• Full keyboard input, plus a hotkey bar for Esc, Tab, Enter, arrows, Del, Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V
• Open a real shell on the remote computer in its own window, with a key row for Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt and the arrows
• Send files to the computer — and take the files it sends back: photos and videos go to your photo library, everything else to the Files app
• Connect once, then choose what to open: its screen, a shell, or file transfer
• Finds the computers that are sharing on your network, and remembers the ones you connect to, with their ping
• Choose which display to watch when the computer is sharing more than one
• Adaptive bitrate and forward error correction keep the stream smooth on shaky Wi-Fi
• A session that loses the network reattaches by itself instead of ending
• Optional clipboard sync: plain text copied on one device can be pasted on the other
• Share this device's screen the other way round, view-only and with the sound your apps play, so a computer can watch it

HOW IT WORKS
Install the free Deskhub app on the computer you want to reach (download from the project page) and start sharing there. Open Deskhub on your device, copy this device's public key from Devices and add it to the computer's list of allowed keys, then add the computer by its address and host key and connect. Once it answers, pick what to open: its screen, a shell, or file transfer. Works on your local network out of the box; for access over the Internet, pair it with Tailscale and connect via your Tailscale IP.

GOOD TO KNOW
Every session is encrypted end to end over QUIC/TLS — video, sound, keystrokes, files, clipboard and everything else. Access is key-based: the computer only lets in a device whose public key its owner has added, and your device only talks to a computer whose host key you have pinned. Nothing is approved over the network, and a key can be revoked from the computer at any time. There are no accounts and no servers of ours: your device talks only to your own computer.

REQUIREMENTS
• A computer running Windows 10 or later (x64), macOS 14 or later, or a current Linux desktop (glibc 2.35+), with the free Deskhub app
• Both devices on the same network, or connected through Tailscale

Deskhub streams the screen of your Windows, macOS or Linux computer to your iPhone or iPad and lets you control it with ultra-low latency — built for gaming, not just remote work.

FEATURES
• Live H.264 video stream with hardware decoding
• Hear what the remote computer is playing, in step with the picture
• Virtual trackpad: drag anywhere to move the mouse, tap to click, double-tap to right-click, hold and drag to select
• Pinch to zoom into the remote screen; a two-finger drag scrolls, or pans once you are zoomed in
• Full keyboard input, plus a hotkey bar for Esc, Tab, Enter, arrows, Del, Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V
• Open a real shell on the remote computer in its own window, with a key row for Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt and the arrows
• Send files to the computer — and take the files it sends back: photos and videos go to your photo library, everything else to the Files app
• Connect once, then choose what to open: its screen, a shell, or file transfer
• Remembers the computers you connect to, with their name and when you last connected, so the next connection is one tap
• Choose which display to watch when the computer is sharing more than one
• Adaptive bitrate and forward error correction keep the stream smooth on shaky Wi-Fi
• A session that loses the network reattaches by itself instead of ending
• Optional clipboard sync: plain text copied on one device can be pasted on the other
• Share this device's screen the other way round, view-only and with the sound your apps play, so a computer can watch it

HOW IT WORKS
Install the free Deskhub app on the computer you want to reach (download from the project page) and press Share there. The quickest way in: press Show QR code on the computer, then Scan QR code in Deskhub on your iPhone or iPad — the computer is trusted and lets this device in at once. Or type the computer's address and connect: compare the fingerprint Deskhub shows with the one on the computer's Devices page, trust it, and have the computer's owner press Approve under Connection requests — this device keeps trying for two minutes. Once it is in, pick what to open: its screen, a shell, or file transfer. Works on your local network out of the box; for access over the Internet, pair it with Tailscale and connect via your Tailscale IP.

GOOD TO KNOW
Every session is encrypted end to end over QUIC/TLS — video, sound, keystrokes, files, clipboard and everything else. Access works like SSH: the computer only lets in a device its owner has let in on purpose — by pressing Approve, by showing it the QR code, or by pasting its public key — and your device remembers each computer's host key after you confirm it the first time; if that key ever changes, Deskhub refuses to connect. A device can be removed from the computer at any time. There are no accounts and no servers of ours: your device talks only to your own computer.

REQUIREMENTS
• A computer running Windows 10 or later (x64), macOS 14 or later, or a current Linux desktop (glibc 2.35+), with Deskhub 9 or newer installed — free from the project page
• Both devices on the same network, or connected through Tailscale

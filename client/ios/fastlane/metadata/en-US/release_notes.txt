• Deskhub 7 signs in with keys, the way SSH does. Passcodes and pairing are gone, and a 7.x device cannot connect to a 6.x one, so update every device
• Earlier pairings are not carried over: allow each client again. Your settings start from their defaults once
• To connect: on this device open Devices → My keys and press Copy public key beside "default". On the computer, paste it under Devices → Clients allowed to connect, then press Allow. Back here, type the computer's address, check the fingerprint against its host key, and press Trust and connect
• The device name now lives in Settings and labels your key

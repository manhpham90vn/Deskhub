Deskhub's first release on the App Store.

• Stream the screen of your Windows, macOS or Linux computer to your iPhone or iPad with low latency, and hear its sound in step with the picture
• Control it with a virtual trackpad and a full keyboard, open a shell on it, and send files both ways
• Connect in seconds: scan the QR code the computer shows, or type its address and have its owner press Approve
• Everything travels encrypted end to end between your own devices — no accounts, no servers
• The computer needs Deskhub 9 or newer, free from the project page

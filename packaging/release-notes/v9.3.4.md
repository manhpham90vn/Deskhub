## Nothing to redo

Deskhub 9.3.4 speaks the same authentication version as every 9.x release, so hosts and
clients on any 9.x release talk to each other and no device has to be let in again.
Coming from 8.x or older, read the
[9.0.1 notes](https://github.com/manhpham90vn/Deskhub/releases/tag/v9.0.1) first: every
device must be on Deskhub 9.

The fix below is on the Mac you connect **to**; the viewer needs nothing new.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **A Mac with *Allow access before anyone signs in* on opens Deskhub again after a
  restart.** 9.3.3 installed from the DMG or through Homebrew kept macOS's quarantine mark
  on the daemon behind that setting. At the next restart macOS tried to verify the daemon
  online before the network was up, could not, stopped it, and from then on greeted the
  app itself with *Apple could not verify "Deskhub" is free of malware* until it was
  allowed by hand in System Settings; reinstalling did not help. The app now clears that
  mark from the daemon when the setting is ticked and at every launch while it is on, so
  the daemon starts at boot without a check and the app opens as usual. A Mac already
  allowed by hand needs nothing more.
- **The daemon comes back after a Homebrew reinstall.** Reinstalling the cask removed the
  daemon from launchd while macOS still listed it as allowed, and the app waited for a
  daemon that was not there. The app now registers it again at every launch while the
  setting is on.

## Nothing to redo

Deskhub 9.3.1 speaks the same authentication version as every 9.x release, so hosts and
clients on any 9.x release talk to each other and no device has to be let in again.
Coming from 8.x or older, read the
[9.0.1 notes](https://github.com/manhpham90vn/Deskhub/releases/tag/v9.0.1) first: every
device must be on Deskhub 9.

The fixes below are on the Mac you connect **to**; the viewer needs nothing new.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **A Mac can now be signed into from the login window.** With *Allow access before
  anyone signs in* on, 9.3.0 showed a viewer the login window but swallowed its mouse and
  keyboard, and the first click froze the picture until someone signed in at the Mac. The
  host before sign-in now posts input through IOKit, the route macOS keeps open at the
  login window, and never freezes.
- **A Mac set to start Deskhub at login and share on launch shares again without a
  click.** When the app had been living in the menu bar at the previous shutdown, macOS
  brought it back at login without a window, and the share that waited for that window
  never started: the Mac answered at the login window, then went quiet the moment someone
  signed in, until the window was opened by hand. The app now starts that share on its
  own and opens its window at launch, as the settings promise.
- **A Mac that is sharing stays reachable through Log Out.** The app used to quit the
  moment macOS began logging out, while a terminal with a running process or an unsaved
  document was still asking whether to quit, leaving the viewer unable to answer. The share
  now stays up until every other app has quit; if the log-out is cancelled on the Mac, the
  app keeps running.
- **Fewer round trips to the daemon.** While waiting for a display at login, the app sent
  its *access before sign-in* copies to the daemon on every half-second probe; it now
  sends them only when the settings or the allowed list change, when a share stops and
  once at launch.

## 📝 Documented

- *Allow access before anyone signs in* on macOS answers at the login window only over an
  address the Mac already has there: a VPN whose client lives in the user's session, such
  as the Tailscale app, is not up before sign-in — use the local network, or run the VPN
  as a system daemon (`tailscaled`).
